# Abstract

Perovskite solar cells (PSCs) are a leading renewable energy technology, yet their long-term stability is limited by moisture and heat. We report a fluorinated hole transport layer that suppresses ion migration at the interface. The champion device reaches a power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 23.4% and retains 90% of its initial efficiency after 1000 h at 85 °C. These results demonstrate that interfacial fluorination is an effective strategy for stable devices [4, 7].

# Introduction

Perovskite solar cells have made remarkable progress over the past decade [1-3], with efficiencies rising from 3.8% to 26% [2]. However, degradation under operating conditions still prevents commercialization. Many studies attribute this degradation to iodide ion migration, which accelerates above 60 °C [5].
